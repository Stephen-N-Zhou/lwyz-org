HOA Mail Summarizer

Exploring a clearer way to organize notices and follow-up items.

The idea

Turn unstructured notices into concise summaries that are easier to review, organize, and track. The concept combines email processing, structured extraction, and reporting.

This page describes a project idea. Source code and a public download are not currently available here.

Design principles

  • Keep sensitive information protected.
  • Make extracted details easy to verify.
  • Present results in a clear, useful format.
