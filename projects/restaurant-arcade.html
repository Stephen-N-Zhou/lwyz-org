How to play

  1. Move the bowl left and right beneath falling ingredients.
  2. Follow the recipe strip from left to right. The glowing gold circle shows the only ingredient you should catch next.
  3. Wrong ingredients, missed recipe ingredients, and chili bombs each cost one heart.
  4. Clear each level to advance. The goal grows harder across 10 levels, and later levels unlock more power-ups.

Move with ← → or A / D, or drag on the game. Use Recipe Peek once to reveal the next ingredient and slow falling food.

Choose an available power-up below and use it with the button or Shift. Beat levels to earn charges; new powers unlock as you progress.
