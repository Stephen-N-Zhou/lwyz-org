Avatar Arcade

Catch ingredients, match dishes, and race through the Forbidden Bite kitchen. Then flap through lantern gates, guide the dumpling snake through an exit gate, break tea bricks, platform-hop across Chinese rooftops, jump spikes and dodge stompers, and stack mooncakes. Play the 100-level campaigns or tap Endless Run in Wok Flyer, Mooncake Stack, Lantern Snake, and Arcade Ace to keep going until the run ends. Dish Match lets you choose your board size. Every game also has power-ups, a daily challenge, and badges.

Offline tip: open Forbidden Bite once while connected on this device. The arcade and both menus will be saved here for later offline access. If the school network blocks a first visit, ask the school to approve the site.

How to play

Read the full instructions
  1. Move the bowl under any falling food. On a phone, touch where you want the bowl to go and slide your finger to steer; on a computer, use ← → or A / D.
  2. Every food item counts. Catch the number shown in the level meter to finish the round. There is no ingredient order to memorize.
  3. Avoid red chili bombs. A caught bomb costs one heart; missed food is harmless. You have 3 hearts, and clearing a level restores one.
  4. Difficulty keeps rising through 100 levels. Every tenth round is a boss fight; defeat all ten bosses to win.

Move with ← → or A / D, or touch and drag the bowl. Use Soup Slowdown with the power-up button or Shift.

Soup Slowdown slows falling items, Ingredient Magnet pulls food toward your bowl, and Wok Sweep clears chili bombs. New powers unlock as you progress.
