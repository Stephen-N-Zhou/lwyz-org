FOOD & DESIGN / CREATIVE CONCEPT

Forbidden Bite

A playful restaurant concept where comfort meets a touch of elegance.

This is a fictional creative concept, not an operating restaurant.

The inspiration

Chinese flavors, warm hospitality, and a little imagination come together in a menu of noodles, rice bowls, snacks, and sweet endings.

Menu ideas

  • Dragon Fire Noodles — spicy broth, braised beef, and chili crisp.
  • Silken Path — sesame broth, tofu, and mushrooms.
  • Forbidden Popcorn Chicken — crispy five-spice with scallion.
  • Jade Pillows — matcha mochi for a sweet finish.
