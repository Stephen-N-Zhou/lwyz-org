Our story

A little ritual in every bite

Inspired by Chinese flavors and modern hospitality, Forbidden Bite brings together hand-pulled noodles, golden popcorn chicken, nourishing soups, colorful rice bowls, and fresh seafood.

We love the contrast of chili oil over steaming noodles, the crunch of five-spice chicken, and a calming cup of chrysanthemum tea. Each idea balances rich and refreshing, bold and gentle, with a family-friendly spirit.

Concept note: Forbidden Bite is a fictional creative concept. Menu items and imagery are illustrative; this page does not represent an operating restaurant or accept orders.

From the concept kitchen

A story behind every favorite

Little notes about the flavors and ideas on this imagined menu.

Dish story

Dragon Fire Noodles

This bowl is built around contrast: warming chili broth, tender beef, crisp greens, and a bright spoonful of chili crisp. The “dragon fire” name is a playful nod to its bold, adjustable heat.

Dish story

Smashed Cucumbers

Cracking the cucumbers creates plenty of edges for garlic, rice vinegar, sesame, and chili oil to cling to. It’s a cool, tangy first bite designed to wake up the palate.

Dish story

Jade Pillows

These soft matcha mochi are imagined as a gentle finish: earthy green tea on the outside, sweet red bean at the center, and a small nod to the restaurant’s jade-and-gold palette.

Questions & answers

Curious about Forbidden Bite?

Is Forbidden Bite open?

No. Forbidden Bite is a fictional restaurant concept created for food and design exploration.

Can I place an order or make a reservation?

You can try the demo cart and reservation preview, but neither sends a real order or booking. The menu is illustrative and there is no operating location.

What does the picture menu show?

The picture menu is an illustration gallery with no text. The full menu lists dish names, descriptions, prices, and customization choices.

Can I ask a question about the concept?

Yes. Open a public question on GitHub. You can add a reply to the same conversation when you have an answer.

Ask a question on GitHub

GitHub sign-in is required. Questions and replies are public.

Is there a real location or parking?

No. This is an imaginary restaurant, so there is no address, parking, real opening hours, takeout service, or group booking.

Are the seasonal menu and chef picks real offers?

No. They are sample ideas created for the Forbidden Bite concept and are not available to order.

Are the reviews from real customers?

No. The examples are fictional, and visitor entries are pretend reviews posted publicly to this project’s GitHub Issues. A GitHub account is required to submit one.
